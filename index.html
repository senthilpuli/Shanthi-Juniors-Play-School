Frequently Asked Questions

Questions from Cuddalore Parents

Everything you need to know about admissions, programmes, and school life at Shanthi Juniors Play School.

What is the best play school in Cuddalore?
Shanthi Juniors Play School in Manjakuppam, Cuddalore is widely regarded as one of the best nursery and play schools in the area. With over 10 years of experience, 100+ enrolled students, qualified certified educators, and a safe, nurturing campus, we provide exceptional early childhood education for children aged 2–6 years.
What age group does Shanthi Juniors accept?
We welcome children from 2 years old through 6 years old. Our six programmes are: Daycare (2 years), Playgroup (2–3 years), Nursery (2.5–3.5 years), Pre-KG (3–4 years), Junior KG / LKG (4–5 years), and Senior KG / UKG (5–6 years). Each is tailored to your child's developmental stage.
How can I get my child admitted to Shanthi Juniors?
Admissions for 2026–27 are now open. You can call us at 94861 87606 or 96003 37614, WhatsApp us directly, or fill in the online enquiry form on this page. Our admissions team will contact you within one working day. We strongly advise early enquiry as seats are limited across all programmes.
What are the school timings at Shanthi Juniors, Cuddalore?
Shanthi Juniors Play School is open Monday to Saturday, from 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM. The school is closed on Sundays and on public holidays.
Where is Shanthi Juniors Play School located?
We are located at 20, Bashyam Reddy Street, Manjakuppam, Cuddalore – 607 001, Tamil Nadu. We are easily accessible from Cuddalore town and serve families from Manjakuppam, Cuddalore New Town, and nearby areas. View on map →
What curriculum does Shanthi Juniors follow?
We follow a curriculum that integrates Montessori methodology with CBSE framework standards. This ensures children develop independence and curiosity (Montessori) while also building structured academic readiness (CBSE) — preparing them for any primary school in Cuddalore and Tamil Nadu.
